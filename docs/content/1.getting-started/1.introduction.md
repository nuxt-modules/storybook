Nuxt Storybook brings your Nuxt app into [Storybook](https://storybook.js.org): stories render your components with the plugins, auto-imports, components and runtime config of your app. It is made of two packages, which do different jobs.

| | `@storybook-vue/nuxt` | `@nuxtjs/storybook` |
|---|---|---|
| Kind | Storybook framework | Nuxt module |
| Configured in | `.storybook/main.ts` and `.storybook/preview.ts` | `nuxt.config.ts` |
| Used by | `storybook dev`, `storybook build` and `nuxt dev` | `nuxt dev` only |
| Required | Yes | No |

## The Storybook framework

`@storybook-vue/nuxt` is the [Storybook framework](https://storybook.js.org/docs/configure/integration/frameworks) for Nuxt. You set it as the `framework` of `.storybook/main.ts`, and it makes your stories Nuxt-aware:

- It loads your Nuxt config, so stories get the Vite config, plugins, CSS, auto-imports and components of your app.
- Each story renders in its own Nuxt app, which you configure with [`parameters.nuxt`](/storybook/writing-stories).
- It mocks auto-imports, components and server endpoints in stories with [`mockNuxtImport`, `mockComponent` and `registerEndpoint`](/storybook/mocking).
- It exports `defineMain` (from `@storybook-vue/nuxt/node`) and `definePreview`, to write your [configuration](/storybook/config) with [CSF Factories](https://storybook.js.org/docs/api/csf/csf-next).

The framework works on its own: `storybook dev` and `storybook build` only need it.

## The Nuxt module

`@nuxtjs/storybook` is the Nuxt module that makes Storybook available through your Nuxt server.
Add it to the `modules` of `nuxt.config.ts` to:

- Serve Storybook from `nuxt dev`, on the same host and port as your app, under `/_storybook/`.
- Open Storybook in a [Nuxt DevTools tab](/nuxt-module/viewer).
- Include `.storybook/` in the TypeScript config Nuxt generates.

It has a few [options](/nuxt-module/options), such as the `route` Storybook is served on. The module depends on the framework, and does nothing during `nuxt build` or `storybook build`.

## Which one do I need?

- **Storybook with Nuxt:** the framework. [Setup](/getting-started/setup) installs it with Storybook.
- **Storybook in `nuxt dev` and the Nuxt DevTools:** the module as well.

::tip{type="info"}
Your project imports `@storybook-vue/nuxt` in `.storybook/` and in your stories, so install it next to `@nuxtjs/storybook` even when you use the module.
::
